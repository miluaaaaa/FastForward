{\bfseries Reporting scope and reproducibility.}\par
All tables are generated from archived result summaries. The Qwen main cohort is kept separate from earlier standalone measurements and delivered plugin versions. Other families are supplementary transfer/adaptation studies; joint changes do not identify individual component effects. Historical reports, including invalid or superseded runs, are retained for traceability rather than counted as additional accepted results. No new GPU measurements were performed for this formatting revision. Per-table precision follows the available source records; missing latencies are not reconstructed from rounded ratios.

The accompanying source manifest records the input hashes. The underlying model weights, checkpoints, and per-example predictions remain in their existing execution storage. This document reports scoped forced-choice visual question answering results, not unrestricted text-generation performance or a universally interchangeable plugin.
